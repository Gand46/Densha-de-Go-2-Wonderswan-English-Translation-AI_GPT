# Technical findings and text consumers — v0.4.17-test

This is a source and evidence index for the attached checkpoint. Hexadecimal `0x...` resource addresses below are **ROM file offsets** as recorded in the resource inventory and build scripts. `F000:...` and `E000:...` are **segmented execution addresses** from runtime traces. `0x...` WRAM and tilemap addresses retain their reported address spaces. No conversion among these forms is assumed. Unless a row cites a runtime trace, a resource offset alone does not identify its consumer.

## Release gate and provenance

`translations/TRANSLATION_LEDGER.json` covers five recent fixes and uses both `linguistic_reviewed` and `linguistic_approved`; it does not provide a complete historical linguistic census. The synthetic end-branch middle dialogue balloon is empty in both tested Japanese and English access paths; that observation does not validate its natural context. A separate `0x3D90C0` notice attempt timed out without a trace/screenshot; no corruption conclusion follows. Coupling/PASS RANGE, inherited abbreviations (including AKITA SHINKAN), environmental lettering and train photo destinations remain open. Global linguistic and visual approval are `NEEDS_REVIEW`, and this version remains a test prerelease.

The recorded clean build and emulator checks are **historical**: their original Japanese ROM, full private dumps and full environmental gallery are absent from this public source package. The packaged representative images and synthetic traces retain the narrower claims above. `docs/VALIDACION_V0417.md`, `docs/PRE_RC_AUDIT.md`, `docs/SYNTHETIC_QA_V0417.md` and `qa/reports/` preserve the source reports. Historical reports contain paths into a private checkpoint that are not resolvable in this public package; they are retained as provenance, not presented as included artifacts.
