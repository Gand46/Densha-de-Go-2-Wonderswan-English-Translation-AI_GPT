# Known issues and evidence gaps

This is a playable TEST prerelease, not a certified complete translation.

- Environmental signage: 2,915 resources extracted; 204 individually sampled visually; 2,711 still lack individual review. These are graphics, not 2,711 confirmed Japanese texts.
- Five bank-02 train photos have small destination displays requiring reliable reading and context.
- `AKITA SHINKAN` and other inherited abbreviations need terminology review.
- Bonus/coupling variants, rare notices and performance-dependent endings need additional contextual tests.
- `RUN ENDED` is repaired and verified in its actual consumer via assisted substate access. Natural full completion has not yet been demonstrated for this screen.
- The recent-fixes ledger covers five entries, not the entire historical translation. `linguistic_reviewed` does not automatically mean `linguistic_approved`.
- Windows/macOS execution and physical hardware have not been tested for this repository packaging.

All six courses are available through normal left/right page navigation. They are not progression-locked. Selector indices 2 and 3 are exchanged internally after confirmation; QA must verify the selected course.

No new gameplay blockers were observed within the documented tests. Coverage outside those tests remains unknown. Do not convert these unknowns into zero issues or 100% completion.
